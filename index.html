5. Supply, pillow price ladder and cannibalisation

Spare capacity decides where to push. Chairs and solid wood have room; engineered wood is at its limit.

Pillow sales by price band (Amazon, ₹ Cr est.)

Online-only entry pillow: cannibalisation check

Net = entry units × entry margin per unit, minus cannibalised units × premium margin per unit. Fence the entry pillow: online only, a separate name, a different pack or fill, no store or MBO listing. The premium price is Wakefit's best-selling Amazon pillow 2-pack (₹874); margins are assumptions to replace. Value logic: entry buyers judge value against what they paid, so we expect about 4★ even at the lower price, while pillows selling at ₹200 to 400 each rate only 3.6 to 3.8★.

Blinkit pillow bundles: fees, price per pillow and perceived value

Blinkit charges a flat fee per unit line plus storage for the days a pack sits in the dark store. Bundling spreads the flat fee over more pillows. Each pack is priced to a target discount against buying singles on Blinkit, rounded to a price ending in 49 or 99. At about ₹275 a pillow the flat fee is 17% of a single's price, so on Blinkit the packs are the product.

Amazon pillow packs: what the market does

Price index is the median price per pillow in the under ₹700 band (single = 100). Wakefit's own 2-packs sell about 2.7x its single pillows on Amazon.

Bundles to launch after the range expands

Storage per pack scales as pack size to the power 0.85, because bundles pack more compactly. The ₹50 flat fee is the Blinkit fee as stated by the team; storage, margin and the target discounts are assumptions to replace with your rate card.

6. Method and sources

Our data build: rows and data points pulled with an in-house marketplace analytics tool from Amazon, Flipkart and Blinkit. Amazon product data (466,417 listings) gives sales estimates, price, rating, rank and 30-day growth. Amazon search data (59,407 terms) gives volume, growth, click share, conversion and the top-3 clicked products. Flipkart brand, sub-category and keyword panels and Blinkit search panels supply the rest. Sales values are modelled estimates, so use them for comparison and not as company-reported revenue.

Company figures (channel split, utilisation, stores) come from Wakefit's IPO documents: IDBI Capital note, GEPL note, DRHP.

The keyword list holds the 28 highest-volume terms in each of nine categories (252 terms) after removing covers, kids, toys and accessories.
